How we work

From a first note to the trailhead

Four steps, no payment to start, and every number written down before you commit.

You tell us roughly what you want

Dates or a month, who is travelling, how high you are happy to sleep and anything you must not miss. The plan form takes about two minutes; a WhatsApp message or email works too.

A planner sends a first plan within one working day

Day by day, with the place and sleeping height of every night, the transport for each leg, the stays we suggest and an itemised price in US dollars per person.

We change it until it fits

Add a rest day, swap a flight for a road, move the trip to catch a festival. Each version comes with a new price, so you always see what a change costs.

You book with a deposit

Only when you are ready. We confirm flights, permits, guides and stays in writing, then send a final briefing before you land. Payment terms are set out in our payments policy.

What is in a quote

Every line, written down

Guides and porters

Government-licensed guides, and porters where the route needs them, with their wages, food, lodging and insurance on separate lines.

Permits and fees

National park, conservation area, municipality and restricted-area permits at the rate in force when we quote. Rules change; check current status before you travel.

Stays and transport

The hotels, lodges and teahouses we name, private vehicles, domestic flights and the weather buffer days for mountain flights.

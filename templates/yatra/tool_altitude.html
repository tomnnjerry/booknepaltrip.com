Rules of thumb

  • Above 3,000 m, raise your sleeping height by no more than 300–500 m a day.
  • Take a rest day for every 1,000 m or so you climb.
  • Headache with nausea, dizziness or poor sleep at height: stop climbing.
  • If symptoms get worse, go down. Descent is the treatment.
